About Skeedor

Crafting memories

Skeedor Photos is Bauchi's premier wedding photographer, built on one belief — that your wedding day deserves to be remembered exactly as it felt. Warm, cinematic, and deeply personal. Every frame is crafted to last a lifetime.

With years of experience documenting love stories across Nigeria and beyond, Skeedor brings a calm, unobtrusive presence to every celebration — so you stay in the moment while we capture it. From intimate weddings to grand traditional ceremonies, no love story is too big or too small.
